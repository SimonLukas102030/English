Nature & Environment

The Great Outback

The vast, remote, arid interior of Australia. It covers most of the continent but is home to very few people. Temperatures can exceed 40°C.

Unique Ecosystems

Isolation for millions of years led to the evolution of unique marsupials. However, invasive species and bushfires remain a constant threat.

Society & Economy

Australia is one of the world's most urbanized countries, with 86% of the population living in coastal cities.

Major Exports

Iron ore, coal, gold, and agricultural products like wool and wheat. Mining is a pillar of the Australian economy.

Indigenous Culture

Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples have a continuous history dating back over 65,000 years.
